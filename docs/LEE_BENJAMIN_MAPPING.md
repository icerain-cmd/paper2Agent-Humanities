# Lee–Benjamin Source Mapping

This artifact maps Lee Yong-wook's 2019 paper to separately verified and user-approved Benjamin V2/V3 facsimiles. The mapping was prepared before dialogue execution and then retained as the source-alignment basis for the approved V2/V3 Paper Agents.

## Mapping rule

A Korean quotation or paraphrase is not labelled `EXACT` merely because its idea clearly corresponds to the German source. `EXACT` would require edition-controlled text identity or a verified translation alignment. The current mappings are therefore `STRONG_MATCH` when the cited Korean passage and the German edition-specific passage align strongly in concept, citation context, and page/version identity.

## Edition boundary

The first/second-technology mapping is **V2 only**. V3 was negatively verified for the V2-specific strings and cannot satisfy that mapping. The remaining mappings above use V3 final-version evidence.
