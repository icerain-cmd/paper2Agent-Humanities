# Scholarly dialogue protocol

- COMPARE: describe documented overlap/difference; no winner.
- QUESTION: target a paper or statement and preserve uncertainty.
- CRITIQUE: identify the target and separate criticism from source claims.
- RESPOND: a Paper Agent may cite only its own paper evidence.
- SYNTHESIZE: use AI_SYNTHESIS with derived_from across papers.
- IDENTIFY_GAP: preserve the gap as synthesis or UNRESOLVED.
- GENERATE_RESEARCH_QUESTION: derive from documented tensions/gaps; never attribute the question to either author.
